SIDELINEIQ v0.42.7

PENALTY ANALYTICS
- Analytics now shows team Total Flags and Penalty Yards.
- Flags count every recorded penalty (accepted or declined).
- Penalty Yards count accepted penalty yards only.
- Penalties are attributed to the correct team based on Offense/Defense at the time of the foul.
- Player-attributed penalties appear in a Penalties table:
  Player | Flags | Yards | Penalty Detail.
- Clicking a player opens Player Statistics with a Penalties card showing:
  total flags, total yards, and penalty-type breakdown.
- Next Play / succeeding-spot penalties are retained in penaltyHistory so they remain in analytics
  after enforcement, including PAT/2PT and cross-period penalties.
- Existing v0.42.6 data migrates forward.

Note: historical penalties recorded before v0.42.7 are still calculated from their play context.
